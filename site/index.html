How this site works

Sourced

Every Qur’an passage shows surah and verse. Every hadith shows its collection and number, with a link to check it.

Honest

Where a hadith is not from the two Ṣaḥīḥ collections, the grading is stated. Partial verses are marked as partial.

Gentle

Each page opens with a short “Start here”. Faith and professional help go together, and crisis support is one click away.

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ “Truly, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find peace.” (Qur’an 13:28)

Sakīna was made with the intention of comfort. It is a collection of references, not a source of fatwā or medical advice. Please verify any reference on Quran.com and Sunnah.com and ask qualified scholars about religious rulings.
