About the prayers

…

And when you have completed the prayer, remember Allah standing, sitting, or [lying] on your sides. But when you become secure, re-establish [regular] prayer. Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers at specified times.

Prayer has appointed times, which is why accurate times matter.

Qur’an 4:103 Sūrat An-Nisāʾ

Ibn Masʿūd asked the Prophet ﷺ: “Which deed is most beloved to Allah?” He said: “Prayer at its proper time.” He asked: “Then what?” He said: “Kindness to parents.” He asked: “Then what?” He said: “Jihad in the cause of Allah.”

Narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him)

The first answer is the one to remember: prayer on time.

Sahih al-Bukhari 527 Sahih Muslim 85Ṣaḥīḥ

The Prophet ﷺ asked: “What do you think if there were a river at the door of one of you in which he bathed five times every day; would any dirt remain on him?” They said, “No dirt would remain.” He said: “That is like the five prayers; by them Allah erases sins.”

Narrated by Abū Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him)

Sahih al-Bukhari 528 Sahih Muslim 667Ṣaḥīḥ

About these times

Times come from the free AlAdhan prayer-times service. Countries and communities use different calculation methods, and some masjids follow their own timetable. If the times here differ from your local masjid, please follow your masjid. Your chosen city is saved only in this browser.
