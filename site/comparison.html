Comparison & Low Self-Esteem

When everyone online seems to be doing better than you

Social media shows highlight reels. The Qur’an and the Prophet ﷺ speak directly to the sickness of comparing: your worth with Allah is not measured in followers, looks or lifestyle.

To feel better: do this

Don’t try everything. Pick one that feels doable, and do it now. Fixed numbers are given only where the hadith gives them; otherwise say it as often as you like.

Name three blessings and say Alḥamdulillāh

Before you move on, say out loud three specific things you have. Gratitude changes where your attention sits.

Gentle, practical steps

Small things you can try today. Not religious rulings, just ordinary wellbeing help that sits well alongside the guidance above.

  1. Mute or unfollow accounts that leave you feeling smaller. Your heart is not a content farm.
  2. Each night write three specific blessings you have. Gratitude trains attention.
  3. Compare yourself only to yourself of last year: are you closer to Allah, kinder, more skilled?
  4. Invest in one real, offline friendship or skill this week.
