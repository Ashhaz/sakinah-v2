Stress & Anxiety

When your mind will not stop racing

Worry is a very human experience, and the Qur’an names it directly. Here is what Allah and His Messenger ﷺ say to a restless heart.

To feel better: do this

Don’t try everything. Pick one that feels doable, and do it now. Fixed numbers are given only where the hadith gives them; otherwise say it as often as you like.

Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh

Say it whenever you feel you don’t have the strength. It means admitting that your strength comes from Allah.

لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.

There is no power and no strength except with Allah.

“Shall I not tell you of a treasure from the treasures of Paradise? It is: lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.” (Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī (may Allah be pleased with him))

Sahih al-Bukhari 6384 Sahih Muslim 2704

Remember Allah with the four beloved phrases

Breathe slowly and say any of these quietly, as many times as feels right. There is no fixed number, so the counters are only a help.

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، وَلَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ

SubḥānAllāh, wal-ḥamdu lillāh, wa lā ilāha illallāh, wallāhu akbar.

Glory be to Allah; praise be to Allah; there is no god but Allah; Allah is the Greatest.

“The most beloved words to Allah are four: SubḥānAllāh, Alḥamdulillāh, Lā ilāha illallāh and Allāhu akbar. It does not matter which one you begin with.” (Samurah ibn Jundub (may Allah be pleased with him))

Also: “Truly, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find peace” (13:28), and the Prophet ﷺ said the one who remembers his Lord and the one who does not are like the living and the dead (Bukhari 6407).

Pour your heart out in sujūd

Pray two rakʿahs, or your next prayer, and make a lot of du’a in prostration. Tell Allah everything that is on your heart.

The closest a servant is to his Lord is when he is prostrating, so make abundant du’a. (Abū Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him))

Sahih Muslim 482

Recite Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ, al-Falaq and an-Nās, three times each, in the morning and in the evening.

From the Qur’an

Those who have believed, and whose hearts find peace in the remembrance of Allah. Truly, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find peace.

Try it now: put the phone down and say “Allāh” or “SubḥānAllāh” slowly, once per breath.

Qur’an 13:28 Sūrat Ar-Raʿd
…

Those to whom people said, “Indeed, the people have gathered against you, so fear them.” But it increased them in faith, and they said, “Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs.”

When fear is loud, answer it with: ḥasbunallāhu wa niʿmal-wakīl.

Qur’an 3:173 Sūrat Āl ʿImrān
…

Allah does not charge a soul except [with that within] its capacity. It will have [the consequence of] what [good] it has gained, and it will bear [the consequence of] what [evil] it has earned. “Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we have forgotten or erred. Our Lord, and lay not upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us. Our Lord, and burden us not with that which we have no ability to bear. And pardon us; and forgive us; and have mercy upon us. You are our Protector…”

You will never be handed more than you can carry. If it feels like too much, ask for help from people and from Allah.

Qur’an 2:286 Sūrat Al-Baqarah
…

Say, “Never will we be struck except by what Allah has decreed for us; He is our protector.” And upon Allah let the believers rely.

Much anxiety is about outcomes. This verse hands the outcomes back to the One who holds them.

Qur’an 9:51 Sūrat At-Tawbah
…

So by mercy from Allah, [O Muhammad], you were lenient with them. And if you had been rude [in speech] and harsh in heart, they would have disbanded from about you. So pardon them and ask forgiveness for them and consult them in the matter. And when you have decided, then rely upon Allah. Indeed, Allah loves those who rely [upon Him].

The pattern: think, plan, consult, decide – and then rely on Allah. Do your part, then let go of the rest.

Qur’an 3:159 Sūrat Āl ʿImrān
…

O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient.

Two tools in one verse: sabr and ṣalāh.

Qur’an 2:153 Sūrat Al-Baqarah
…

[Allah] said, “Fear not. Indeed, I am with you both; I hear and I see.”

Allah’s words to Mūsā and Hārūn ﷺ as they walked toward something terrifying.

Gentle, practical steps

Small things you can try today. Not religious rulings, just ordinary wellbeing help that sits well alongside the guidance above.

  1. Slow your breathing: in for 4, out for 6, for one minute. Use the breathing circle above.
  2. Write the worry down. Then circle the part you can act on today, and leave the rest with Allah.
  3. Move your body for ten minutes. Even a short walk helps with restlessness.
  4. Cut back on caffeine and late-night scrolling for a week and notice the difference.
  5. If anxiety is stopping you from living normally, speak to a doctor or counsellor.
